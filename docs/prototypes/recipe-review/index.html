Reviewing a parsed recipe

A review-first import of a 17-line, two-section recipe: every Jev decision is flagged. Can a household clear it in a few taps without rubber-stamping the lines that are genuinely wrong? Switch variants, clear the review, and compare taps and time.

Verdict

  • Variant A (in recipe order, tiered) wins. Recipe order keeps the context that makes a line checkable (which section, what came before), and the two densities do the triage that variant C needed two piles for.
  • Two tiers. Check lines (any check flag other than review-first) open as cards with the question, the highlighted span and up to three one-tap alternatives. Glance lines (only review-first flags) are one compact row: original text over the engine's reading.
  • Answering a card's question confirms the rest of that line, so a check line costs one tap when an alternative is right.
  • One bulk action, glance lines only. "Looks right - confirm N" never touches a check line or a below-gate tag. Bulk confirmations are stored with method bulk and are not golden-set candidates.
  • From cold, A clears this recipe in 7 taps (tags, effort, bulk, four answers). B needs the same 7 only if the bulk shortcut is used; otherwise it is 17 cards of "Right, Right, Right" with one line of context. C is as fast as A but reorders the recipe, so duplicate and section questions lose their context.
