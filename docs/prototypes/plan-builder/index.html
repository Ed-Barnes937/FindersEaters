Scenario

Resets the plan. The worked example matches section 8 of the scoring spec in shape; scores differ because this library has 24 recipes, not 60.

Pending suggestions, scored against everything else on screen

Contributions sum to the score. Reasons on the cards are recomputed against the current set (plan items plus the other pending suggestions) after every change, so they stay true after a skip; which recipe sits in each slot only changes on that slot's own skip.

Session state

    Locked flow (variant A)

    • One Plan screen: plan items on top (grouped by day once any is dated), suggestions inline underneath as dashed cards. Nothing is in the plan until added.
    • Anchors: "Add recipes" opens the Recipes screen in pick mode; Add to plan also works from a recipe view.
    • Suggest bar: meal type, "how many more" (defaults to 5 minus plan items, at least 1), and the same Filters sheet as Recipes.
    • Card: title, time and effort, at most two positive reasons (overlap names one shared ingredient, or two from the same recipe), "Why this?", then Add, Another, Swap.
    • Reasons on every card are recomputed against the current set after any change, so they stay true and read both ways ("lime with Fish tacos" on both cards).
    • Another = skip: the card is replaced in place, others stay put. Swap = the next five for that slot, or search; the chosen one goes straight into the plan.
    • "Add all" in the footer. Why this? shows every reason as a signed bar, no numbers.
    • Day and servings live on plan items only: a day chip opens a two-week day picker plus "Any day".
